What we do

Our mission

At Avenir, we don’t just offer homes — we support people in building futures.

At Avenir Housing CIC, our mission is to create lasting change by providing safe, stable housing for people experiencing homelessness or housing insecurity. We recognise that a secure home is the foundation for rebuilding lives, restoring independence and improve wellbeing. Through tailored support and collaboration with local authorities, charities and specialist services, we help individuals overcome barriers, build resilience and work towards a more secure independent future.
